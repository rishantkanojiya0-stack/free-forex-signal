RSI (14) —

—
RSI measures momentum on a 0–100 scale. Below 30 is often called "oversold"; above 70 "overbought." These are descriptions, not predictions. Markets can stay oversold for weeks.

A moving average smooths past prices. Price above the average is sometimes called "bullish"; below, "bearish." This is a description of the past, not a forecast.

Volatility (Std Dev)

—
Standard deviation of recent price changes. Higher numbers mean the price is swinging more. Volatility is not direction — it doesn't tell you up or down, only how much movement is happening.
